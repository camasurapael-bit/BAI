The Hotel

A glass landmark
on the Mactan Channel.

Set in a striking contemporary tower between Cebu City and Mandaue, bai Hotel pairs cosmopolitan design with genuine Filipino warmth — from its soaring golden lobby to the rooftop pool overlooking the channel.

You're never far from anything: 4 km to the Cebu ferry terminal, 6 km to Magellan's Cross, and 8 km from the international airport.
